Illustrative teaching model of a carbon-in-pulp (CIP) gold circuit, not a drawing of a specific plant. Spartan Gold Engineering supplies CIP and CIL plants from 20 to 500 tpd; the number of tanks, sizes and equipment depend on the design. Times are compressed from hours and days to seconds. Concentrations and flows are typical values. Cyanide, acid and mercury are hazardous and must only be handled by trained staff.
